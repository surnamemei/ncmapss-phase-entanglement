Please consider my manuscript, “Auditing Flight-Phase Dependence in Unsupervised Aero-Engine Anomaly Detection: A Discovery-and-Confirmation Study on N-CMAPSS,” as a research article for *Reliability Engineering & System Safety*.

Reliability monitoring depends not only on whether an anomaly score distinguishes health states in aggregate, but also on whether a selected alarm threshold maintains its intended healthy false-alarm behaviour under normal changes in operating context. Full-flight aero-engine trajectories provide a demanding setting for this calibration question: a pooled nominal false-alarm target can coexist with materially different healthy alarm rates in climb, cruise, and descent. The paper audits this issue directly, including cross-phase threshold transfer and engine-aware uncertainty. It does not introduce a new detector or claim that one detector is superior.

The study uses N-CMAPSS DS02 as the exploratory discovery subset. It compares three tested detector implementations—PCA, Isolation Forest, and causal LSTM sequence reconstruction—and examines the sensitivity of the observed phase dependence to specified operating-condition corrections. The analysis protocol, primary endpoints, and DS03 evaluation procedure were then frozen before official DS03 test access. I applied that protocol once to DS03 as an independent confirmatory subset. The pre-specified pooled directional pattern was reproduced, while the manuscript retains the engine- and seed-level exceptions and uncertainty qualifications. The study is limited to simulated data and retrospective healthy false-alarm evaluation; it makes no claim of real-aircraft operational validation or detector independence.

The work is relevant to the journal's readership because alarm calibration, context transfer, and uncertainty at the engine level bear directly on the evaluation of automated fault-monitoring systems. Its practical contribution is an auditable way to assess whether a nominal healthy false-alarm setting remains stable across normal operating regimes, alongside conventional aggregate detector metrics.

The manuscript is original, has not been published previously, and is not under consideration by another journal. Raw N-CMAPSS data originate from NASA's public distribution and are not redistributed in the repository. The submission package includes the manuscript, figures, supplementary provenance material, and availability statements.

Thank you for considering the manuscript.
