Dear Editor,

Please consider the enclosed research article, “Auditing Flight-Phase Dependence in Unsupervised Aero-Engine Anomaly Detection: A Discovery-and-Confirmation Study on N-CMAPSS,” for *Reliability Engineering & System Safety*.

The paper examines whether a nominal healthy false-alarm calibration remains stable across normal flight phases during full-flight aero-engine operation. This question is relevant to reliability monitoring because an aggregate false-alarm target can conceal context-dependent alarm behaviour at the selected threshold. The study uses N-CMAPSS DS02 for exploratory development and robustness checks, freezes the protocol, and then applies it once to an independent DS03 confirmatory subset. It audits PCA, Isolation Forest, and a causal LSTM sequence-reconstruction implementation; it does not propose a new detector. The conclusions are limited to simulated data and retrospective healthy false-alarm evaluation, without a claim of real-aircraft validation or detector independence.
