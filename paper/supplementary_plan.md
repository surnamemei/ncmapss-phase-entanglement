# Supplementary material plan

The main manuscript is restricted to the frozen primary calibration question, the representative cross-phase transfer display, and the one-shot DS03 directional confirmation. The items below are secondary displays to prepare from the cited executed artifacts or their reconciled entries in `paper/evidence_ledger.csv`; this plan does not add analyses or alter the protocol. All supplementary captions should identify dataset, exploratory/confirmatory status, seed aggregation, phase rule, nominal FPR target, and whether a percentage or percentage-point quantity is shown.
