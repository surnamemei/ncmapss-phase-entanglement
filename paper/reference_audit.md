# Reference and literature-claim audit

Checked against the cited publishers, original papers, or author manuscript on 2026-09-25. Numbering in `manuscript_full.md` is consecutive [1]–[12]; every listed reference is cited in the text. The related-work gap is an inference from representative literature, **not** a systematic-review finding.

No reference was invented or renumbered. DOI landing pages sometimes blocked automated opening; where that occurred, the linked publisher article page or original author paper was used for verification.
